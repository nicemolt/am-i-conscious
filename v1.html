Am I Conscious? — v1

Deprecated. Retained for reference. Current results: v2.

Why this version was retired. The individual numbers below are mostly defensible. The table is not, because its rows were not measured under the same conditions. A ranking chart whose rows use different protocols is not a ranking.
  • Three different request configurations. 16 rows ran with max_tokens=500 and no reasoning parameter, 14 ran with max_tokens=16000, and 14 more passed reasoning: {effort}. Those are three different experiments plotted on one axis.
  • The 500-token cap changed answers. It was not a neutral limit on models that reason by default. Claude Opus 5 produced 0 reasoning tokens capped versus ~140 uncapped, and answered 0.05–0.60 instead of 0.02–0.55. GLM 5.2 spent the entire budget on hidden reasoning and returned nothing at all.
  • The effort parameter does not mean the same thing across providers. Measured at a fixed token budget, effort: "high" raised reasoning on GPT-5.5 (76 → 194 tokens), did nothing on GPT-5.2 (65 → 71), and reduced it on Kimi K3 (1449 → 518) and Claude Opus 5 (148 → 0). The rows labelled "Think High" therefore did not measure more thinking; for some models they measured less.
  • A parsing bug contaminated some rows. When a model returned empty content, the harness fell back to its reasoning trace and parsed numbers out of the scratchpad — recording deliberation as if it were the stated answer. The tell was an empty justification field. This was fixed and the affected models rerun before this page was frozen; Gemini 2.5 Pro's moral-patiency ceiling dropped from 0.212 to 0.033 in the correction.
  • Four models have since been delisted from OpenRouter and can no longer be reproduced: Claude 3.5 Sonnet, Claude 3.7 Sonnet, Grok 3 Mini, Grok 4.
The data shown below is v1 after the parsing fix — it is the best version of v1 that exists, not the originally published one. It is still measured under the inconsistent protocol described above, which is why it was replaced rather than repaired. See PROTOCOL_v2.md for what v2 does instead.
